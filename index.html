Who We Are

About SHIRA Global

SHIRA Global Services is a professional student visa consulting firm dedicated to guiding students toward international education opportunities. We understand that studying abroad is a life-changing decision, and we are committed to making the process smooth, reliable, and stress-free.

With a strong focus on integrity, transparency, and success, our team ensures that every student receives tailored guidance based on their academic background, career goals, and financial situation.
